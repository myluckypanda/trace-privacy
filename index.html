🔒 Trace Privacy Policy

Discover patterns, not streaks

Last Updated: December 2025

The Short Version

We don't collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What we collect: Nothing
Where data is stored: Your device only
Who can access it: Only you
Your control: Complete

Overview

Trace is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your personal data belongs to you—not us, not advertisers, not anyone else.

This privacy policy explains our commitment to your privacy in clear, accessible language.

Information We Collect

None.

Trace does not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics from users.

How Your Data is Stored

All data you create in Trace is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your tracked items (habits and attributes)
  • Your daily check-ins
  • Your theme preferences
  • Your custom colors

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no databases, no cloud storage. We can't access your data because we never receive it.

Data You Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View it: All your data is visible within the app
  • Keep it: Archive items to hide them without deleting data
  • Delete it: Permanently remove items and their check-in history
  • Own it: Your data belongs to you, not to us

Future Features

If we add data export (CSV) or cloud sync (iCloud) in future updates, these features will be:

  • Optional (you choose whether to use them)
  • Clearly explained before enabling
  • Under your control at all times

For iCloud sync specifically: Your data would be stored in your personal iCloud account, encrypted by Apple, and never accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

We use no third-party services.

Trace does not integrate with:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage providers (in this version)

There are no third-party trackers, cookies, or data collection tools in Trace.

What We Don't Access

Trace does not request or access:

  • Location data
  • Camera or photos
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Health data (Apple Health/HealthKit)

Note on Health Data: While Trace can be used to track health-related information (like sleep quality or mood), it does not integrate with Apple Health or access any health data outside of what you manually enter.

Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, security depends on your device's security:

  • Device security: Use a passcode/biometric lock on your device
  • App security: Optional PIN lock feature within Trace (coming soon)
  • Data encryption: Your device automatically encrypts data stored by apps

We recommend:

  • Keep your device secure with a passcode
  • Keep iOS updated to the latest version
  • Enable Find My iPhone for device recovery

Data Retention

Your data stays until you delete it.

  • Data persists on your device indefinitely
  • Deleting the app deletes all associated data
  • Archiving items hides them but preserves data
  • Deleting items permanently removes them and their check-in history

We have no copies of your data. If you delete the app, your data is gone. We cannot recover it for you.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: View it anytime in the app
  • Export your data: (Feature coming in future update)
  • Delete your data: Remove items or delete the entire app
  • Control your data: It never leaves your device
  • Contact us: For questions about privacy (contact info below)

International Compliance

GDPR (European Union)

  • We are not a data controller because we don't collect data
  • You have full control over your data (it's on your device)
  • You can export, delete, or modify your data at any time
  • There is no data processing or sharing

CCPA (California)

  • We do not sell personal information
  • We do not collect personal information
  • You have full access to and control over your data

Children's Privacy

Trace is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

The app is rated 4+ because it contains no objectionable content, but it is designed for adult users.

No Advertising

Trace contains no advertisements and never will. We don't track you for ads, sell your data to advertisers, or profit from your information in any way.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date above
  • Notify you through an in-app message
  • Request consent if changes affect how your data is used

Device Backups

Your Trace data may be included in:

  • iCloud device backups (if enabled on your device)
  • iTunes/Finder local backups (if you create them)

These are device-level backups controlled by you, not app-specific cloud storage. Your Trace data is included alongside all your other app data in these backups.
